Karaoke pitch guides for any song.

PitchLane turns a song you upload into a karaoke-room screen: it separates the singer's voice, draws every note as a bar to follow, and syncs the lyrics word by word. It all runs in your browser, and your songs never leave your device.

Why I built it

Japanese songs have pitch bars. Most songs don't.

The problem
  • In Japanese karaoke rooms, every song shows the melody as pitch bars, so you know exactly which note to hit
  • Most Western songs on YouTube only have lyrics, with no pitch guide at all
  • Learning a song by ear means replaying the same line over and over
→
What PitchLane does
  • Creates the pitch-bar guide for songs that don't have one
  • Lets you hear the full song, just the vocals, or just the backing track to sing over
  • Slows songs down without changing the key, so you can learn hard parts
How it works

From an audio file to a karaoke screen

Five steps run in the browser the first time you open a song. The AI models load once and are reused after that.

1

Upload

Any audio or video file. It stays on the device.

Web Audio
2

Separate the voice

An AI model splits the singer from the music.

Spleeter 2-stem
3

Find the pitch

Three methods vote on each note, which handles harmonies and fast passages.

pYIN · harmonic salience · CREPE
4

Draw the bars

Pitch is cleaned up into note bars with start, end and note name.

Canvas
5

Sync the lyrics

A speech model listens to the vocals and times each word.

NeMo Conformer-CTC
Features

Made for actually practising

Hear it

Full song, vocals or BGM

Switch to the backing track only and sing the part yourself, karaoke style.

Learn it

Guide melody and slow-down

Play the bars as a piano melody, and slow to 0.75× or 0.5× without changing the key.

Fix it

Edit the bars

A piano-roll editor highlights bars the app is unsure about and suggests notes it missed.

Read it

Lyrics, three ways

Auto-sync to the voice, paste timed LRC lyrics, or tap along. Japanese furigana supported.

Keep it

Export

Download the vocals, the backing track (WAV), the pitch guide (MIDI) and the lyrics (LRC).

Private

Nothing leaves your device

No account and no server. Songs are processed and saved only in your own browser.

Try it yourself

Four steps to sing along

  1. Open PitchLane and press Try the demo to see it with a built-in public-domain song.
  2. Upload a song from your device (audio or video). The first time, the AI models take about a minute to download.
  3. Add lyrics by pasting them into Lyrics, then press Sync to the voice.
  4. Switch to BGM only and follow the bars as you sing.
